Discover UMD & Duluth

Why I chose the University of Minnesota Duluth and what I appreciate about the city and surrounding area.

Why I Chose UMD

UMD offers the right blend of academic rigor, supportive faculty, and hands-on opportunities. The campus culture encourages exploration and collaboration, which aligns with how I learn best.

  • Strong programs and approachable professors
  • Opportunities for real-world projects
  • Welcoming, close-knit community

What I Like About Duluth

Duluth’s natural beauty, vibrant local scene, and friendly culture make it a great place to live and learn. From Lake Superior views to local cafes and trails, there’s always something to discover.

  • Access to nature and outdoor activities
  • Supportive local community
  • Inspiring environment for study and growth

Personal Experiences

I really enjoy living with friends. We get along well and have similar interests. I also enjoy going to hockey games with friends. Another highlight of living in Duluth is my job at Texas Roadhouse. It is one of the best jobs I have ever had and I have met some really great people.
